# Clearing Price, Issue 01: OpenAI image ads

## LINKEDIN

My call: by Dec 31, 2026, advertisers outside the US can book ChatGPT image ads in Ads Manager, no sales rep needed.

If I’m right, the seconds people spend waiting on an image become a line in 2027 media plans.

The headline read: OpenAI found one more place to put ads.

On Oct 5, OpenAI said it will test labeled ads beside ChatGPT image generations this month, US only.

ChatGPT ads are bought in Ads Manager. Image pricing wasn’t disclosed. Measurement firms get paid too: OpenAI named 20 partners, DoubleVerify and IAS among them.

What OpenAI is really selling is proof. The featured result: WeightWatchers’ attributed CPA on ChatGPT Ads ran 15.3% under its own benchmark, per DV Rockerbox. One advertiser, no spend disclosed, text ads only.

Why OpenAI moves fast: ads hit a $1B run rate on Aug 31 against a reported $2.5B plan for 2026. Self-serve already runs in Europe, and per OpenAI’s release notes, text ads went from a US test to Canada, Australia and New Zealand in about ten weeks.

My guess: Canada or Australia first. The EU last, now that ChatGPT falls under the DSA as a very large search engine.

I’m wrong if the US test disappoints and images stay managed-service only.
